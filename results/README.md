# Evaluation provenance

Reports at this folder's root and in baseline/ or baseline_groq/ predate the verification repair. They are retained as historical artifacts, not claims about current answer quality.

Fresh local evaluations are stored in runs/2026-10-27-*/. See ../docs/repair-validation.md for their scope, settings, sample sizes, and limitations. Offline workflow completion is not live-model verification. Model-judged correctness, relevance and faithfulness are deliberately unscored in these runs.
